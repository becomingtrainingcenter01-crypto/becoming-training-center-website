# Launch checklist

## Required business details
- Official domain name
- Official business email and phone
- Business address or service area
- Final medical training names, dates, prerequisites, duration, and pricing
- VIP Club price, billing frequency, and included benefits
- Meeting method: Zoom, Google Meet, phone, or in person
- Time zone
- Cancellation, refund, no-show, and rescheduling rules
- Stripe account owned and verified by Becoming Training Center

## Technical connections
- Form delivery
- Live booking calendar
- Stripe Checkout / Payment Links
- Confirmation and reminder emails
- Analytics and consent configuration
- Domain and SSL

## Quality assurance
- Review all French and English content
- Test mobile, tablet, and desktop layouts
- Test keyboard navigation and focus states
- Compress any new images before upload
- Verify every link, form, price, and policy
