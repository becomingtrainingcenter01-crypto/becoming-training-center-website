Privacy policy

This policy explains how information submitted through the website may be collected and used.

Working document to be reviewed before publication.

1. Information collected

The website may collect information you voluntarily provide through contact, booking, enrollment, or waitlist forms, including name, email, phone number, preferred program, and message.

2. Sensitive information

Standard forms should not be used to submit diagnoses, medical records, detailed mental-health information, or other sensitive data.

3. Payments

Online payments will be processed by a secure provider such as Stripe. Becoming Training Center will not ask for passwords, verification codes, or complete banking information.

4. Emails and reminders

Contact details may be used to send confirmations, reminders, program information, and responses to inquiries.

5. Retention and security

Information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal requirements.

6. Contact

Official business contact details and the effective date will be added before public launch.
