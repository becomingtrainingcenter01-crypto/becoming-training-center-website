# Becoming Training Center — Premium Bilingual Website

Static bilingual website in French and English. No monthly website-builder subscription is required.

## Preview
Open `index.html` or serve the folder locally.

## Deployment
Upload the contents of this folder to Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, Vercel static hosting, or another static host.

## Before public launch
- Replace `YOUR-DOMAIN.com` in `sitemap.xml`.
- Connect the contact, newsletter, waitlist, and booking forms to a backend/email service.
- Connect live calendar availability and Stripe Checkout.
- Add official contact details, cancellation/refund policy, medical training catalog, and VIP Club pricing.
- Have privacy and terms pages reviewed for the business and its jurisdiction.
